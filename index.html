Spend Tracker

A personal credit card spend and budget tracker

What it is

Spend Tracker is a private, personal app built for and used only by its owner, Soumitra Mahakul. It runs on my own computer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads transaction alert emails from my banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis) in my own Gmail account, using read-only access.
  • Shows my credit card spending per billing cycle, budgets, categories and recurring charges.

Privacy

Read the full privacy policy. In short: data stays on my own device, nothing is shared or sold, and Gmail access is read-only.
